Welcome. Thank you for joining our SafeHaven TAY grassroots community presentation. SafeHaven TAY is a developing community technology project focused on transition-age youth, ages eighteen to twenty-four, in and around Skid Row, Los Angeles.

Our mission is to make it easier for young people to find a path toward stability, with fewer unnecessary barriers and more human guidance. Too often, youth must navigate disconnected systems while trying to find housing, health care, treatment, and support.

SafeHaven TAY proposes a low-barrier starting point. A young person should be able to begin asking for help without first providing a permanent address or sharing more sensitive information than is necessary. With consent, a trained navigator or caseworker can help identify needs, verify options, coordinate referrals, and follow up.

Our application prototype includes an intake workflow and a local database design for coordinating services. It is not yet a live clinical or housing placement service. We still need verified provider information, formal partnerships, security review, and careful pilot testing before handling real participant records.

Privacy and dignity are central to this design. Youth records and employee information belong in the protected application, not on this public website. Real medical care must be provided by qualified professionals, with appropriate consent and choice.

Today, we are inviting the grassroots community to help shape priorities. Our Advisory Team can guide ethics and community needs. Our Development Team can test and improve the technology. Our Action Team can support outreach, relationships, and community organizing.

We are seeking practical feedback, organizational partnerships, volunteer participation, and opportunities to test the approach responsibly. Thank you for reviewing SafeHaven TAY. Your experience and community knowledge should help determine what we build next.
